## Anti-job board for engineers

How we work on GitHub, in short. The full rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/candix-com/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Issues**

- Types: `Bug`, `Feature`, `Task`.
- Body: Problem, Impact, Context.
- Status: `New` → `Ready` → `Started`, or `Waiting` on @tavoyne.
- Priority: `Urgent`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`. Effort: under 100, up to 1,000, or over 1,000 changed lines.

**Pull requests**

- One imperative title under 72 characters.
- Description: Problem, What changes, Worth knowing, Reviewing.
- Draft until `/reviewers:review` ends clean. @tavoyne reviews and merges.
